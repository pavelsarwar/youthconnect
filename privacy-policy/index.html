Privacy Policy

Youth Connect Foundation is committed to protecting the digital rights and personal information of our global community.

Effective Date: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Youth Connect Foundation collects, uses, stores, and protects information shared with us through our website, programs, events, volunteer activities, and digital platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you interact with us:

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address and phone number
  • Organization, school, or institution details
  • Volunteer, partnership, or program application information
  • Event registration and participation details
  • Website usage and basic analytics data

2. How We Use Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and communication
  • Manage programs, events, trainings, and volunteer activities
  • Share updates, announcements, and newsletters
  • Improve our website, services, and community engagement
  • Maintain safety, security, and responsible use of our platforms

3. Data Protection

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website may use trusted third-party services such as analytics tools, embedded content, social media integrations, or external forms. These services may collect limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience, analyze website traffic, and enhance website performance.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may only be shared when necessary for program delivery, legal compliance, safety, or with trusted partners directly supporting Youth Connect Foundation activities.

7. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us directly. We will review and respond to reasonable requests in accordance with applicable requirements.

8. Children and Young People

As a youth-focused organization, we are committed to protecting young people’s privacy and promoting safe, responsible, and inclusive digital participation.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
